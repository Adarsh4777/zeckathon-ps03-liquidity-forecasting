## AMFI flow panel

- Coverage calendar: April 2019–September 2026, 90 months.
- Expected category-month cells: 180 (90 months × Liquid and Overnight).
- Four category-month cells are missing/unverified: Liquid and Overnight in May 2021 and July 2021. The canonical pipeline writes an explicit missing-cell CSV and fails if the count is not four.
- The processed input includes row-level `source_url`, `source_type`, `observation_status`, and transcription notes. The pipeline audits duplicates, source references, numeric missingness, and the identity `mobilised − redemptions = net flow`.
- Two cumulative-table residual reconstruction rows have known identity differences: December 2019 Liquid (₹0.03 crore) and January 2021 Overnight (₹0.04 crore). These are documented and retained; discrepancies greater than ₹0.05 crore fail the pipeline.
- Observations are transcribed from browser-readable sources, not independently downloaded raw official source files. A source URL is evidence of provenance, not proof that every transcription has been independently verified.

## Rates and spread definition

The spread is:

`15–91-day Treasury-bill SGL yield − weighted-average call/notice-money rate (WACR)`

It is expressed in percentage points and is a broad market-rate proxy, not a fund-specific realized yield differential. The transcribed T-bill SGL series ends in June 2026, while WACR continues through September 2026. The canonical cash-buffer backtest scores only months where the target-month spread is observed and never forward-fills the missing rate series.

Source pages:
- WACR monthly series: https://measure.thisindianlife.today/data/rates-call-monthly.json
- T-bill SGL monthly series: https://measure.thisindianlife.today/data/rates-tbill-sgl.json

## Model protocol

- Validation window: March 2023–September 2024.
- Test window: October 2024–September 2026.
- Candidate models: Seasonal Naive, additive-trend ETS, AR(1) SARIMAX, Ridge using lagged features, and Ridge using lagged features plus the lagged spread feature.
- Metrics for each category/window use the common set of dates where every candidate has both an actual and a finite prediction. The selected model is the candidate with lowest validation MAE; test results do not determine selection.
- Forecast flow features are restricted to observations through target month `t-2`; the rate spread is lagged through target month `t-3` or earlier.
- Canonical metrics are written by the pipeline. Do not copy old metric values into this report without regenerating and checking the canonical CSVs after a successful run.

## Cash-buffer policy interpretation

The evaluated demand proxy is `max(-net_flow, 0)`. This is a monthly net-outflow proxy, not daily or intramonth redemption demand. Gross redemptions are retained as a separate stress measure because they can be large even when net flows are positive or close to zero.

Buffer decisions use only observations available through the stated forecast cut-off. The target-month observed spread is used only for ex-post yield-drag scoring. The illustrative cost is:

`max(spread, 0) / 100 × buffer × 30 / 365 + 3 × max(net-outflow proxy − buffer, 0)`

The penalty multiplier is an assumption, not an empirically calibrated loss function. Do not interpret the minimum-cost buffer as a real fund's operational optimum. These results are based on the observed monthly spread series; they are not a historical backtest of a single July 2026 rate snapshot.
